Medical AI · Data · Evaluation

Ali Ganjizadeh, MD, MBA

Physician-scientist by training, engineer by practice, businessman at heart.

I work on the parts of medical AI that decide whether a model can be trusted: where the data came from, what the labels really mean, and how a model is evaluated before anyone relies on it.

I’m a Senior Technical Product Manager at HOPPR, which builds foundation models for medical imaging, and a Research Collaborator at the Mayo Clinic Radiology AI & Informatics Lab. I’m also Vice Chair of the SIIM AI Education Committee.
